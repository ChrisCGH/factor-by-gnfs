KEY DECISION POINTS:
═══════════════════

1. Auto-tuning enabled? 
   - Config: ENABLE_AUTO_TUNING = true/false
   - Default: false (safe)

2. Need to adjust?
   - Requires: 10+ samples collected
   - Requires: 5+ samples since last adjustment
   - Trigger: Trend < -0.5 OR Rate < 0.8 × Best

3. Adjustment strategy:
   - Known best: Gradual move (10% steps)
   - Unknown best: Small exploration
   - Always: Safety bounds enforced
